The [LexArt](https://www.lexart.org/) Painting Studio Group invites New
England artists to submit work for our upcoming exhibit.

**At This Moment** celebrates painting in all its forms. The exhibition
welcomes work in all painting media, as well as collage and mixed-media
pieces with a strong painted element. The show is displayed in LexArt's Molly
Harding Nye Gallery, an outstanding museum-style exhibition space. Artists
are also able to opt in to an online exhibit, viewable here on
AtThisMoment.show.

Artwork must be submitted through the LexArt website.
